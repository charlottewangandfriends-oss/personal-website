## ✏️ How to edit your website (text & photos)

Everything on the site — your bio, taglines, photos, videos, writing — can be edited through a
simple admin panel. **You never touch code.**

1. Go to **https://charlottewangmusic.vercel.app/keystatic** in your browser.
2. **Log in with GitHub** when asked (use the `charlottewangandfriends-oss` account).
3. You'll see a list of editable sections in the left sidebar:
   - **Home page** — tagline, the playful subtitle, intro paragraph, hero photos
   - **About page** — your biography, "My Story", the statement, CV upload, portrait
   - **Writing page (intro)** — your writer bio
   - **Contact page** — email + social links
   - **Media — Videos** — add/remove performance videos (just paste a YouTube link)
   - **Photo gallery** — upload/remove photos
   - **Writing pieces** — add poems, stories, essays
4. Click a section, change the text or **drag a new photo** into an image box.
5. Click **Save** (top right).
6. Wait ~30–60 seconds and refresh your website — your change is live. ✨

### Common edits

- **Change a paragraph of text:** open the section, click in the box, type. Save.
- **Replace a photo:** open the section, click the image, upload a new one. Save.
- **Add a performance video:** *Media — Videos → Create*. Give it a title, pick a category
  (Conducting / Composition / Singing / Collaborative Piano), paste the **YouTube link**. Save.
- **Add a writing piece:** *Writing pieces → Create*. Title, category, language, the text. Save.
- **Add your CV:** *About page → CV (PDF) → upload* your PDF. The "Download CV" button appears
  automatically.

> Tip: changes go live automatically because saving "publishes" them. There's no separate
> publish button to worry about.

## 🔑 The accounts behind your website

You don't need these for day-to-day editing, but here's what they are:

### 1. GitHub — where the website's files & content live
- **Account:** `charlottewangandfriends-oss`
- **Repository (the project):** https://github.com/charlottewangandfriends-oss/personal-website
- **What it does:** stores everything. When you Save in the editor, it quietly saves here, which
  triggers the website to rebuild with your changes.
- **Login:** https://github.com/login

### 2. Vercel — what publishes the website to the internet
- **Dashboard:** https://vercel.com/dashboard
- **What it does:** every time content changes on GitHub, Vercel automatically rebuilds and
  publishes the live site within a minute. You rarely need to open this.
- **Login:** sign in with GitHub.

### 3. The domain — charlottewangmusic.com
- Registered with: `<REGISTRAR>` *(GoDaddy / Namecheap / etc.)*
- Points to Vercel so your site shows at charlottewangmusic.com.

## 🆘 If something looks wrong

- **A change didn't show up:** wait a full minute and hard-refresh (Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R).
- **Can't log in to the editor:** make sure you're logged into the `charlottewangandfriends-oss`
  GitHub account.
- **Anything broken or confusing:** contact **Tina** — she set this up and can help.
